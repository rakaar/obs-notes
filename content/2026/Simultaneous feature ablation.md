# Simultaneous feature ablation

How do you remove two overlapping feature directions from a residual-stream activation? Subtracting along a diagonal can restore a horizontal component you just cleared. The worked example follows that component, then derives the edit that makes both dot products zero while preserving the rest of the activation.
