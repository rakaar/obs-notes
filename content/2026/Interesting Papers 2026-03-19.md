- **Uchida — hard-wired circuit**
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12458361/#S2
  - Notes from 2026-04-14 TD scratch notes:
    - Working hypothesis: dopamine in lNAcc is closer to a TD / teaching signal, while D1-MSN population activity may look more like value / reward prediction.
    - D1-MSN activity should not be read like fast glutamatergic excitation; D1 receptors are modulatory rather than directly spike-driving.
    - Open question: why visual cue-related D1 activity reflects upcoming reward prediction, but direct reward / opto-triggered dopamine does not simply produce a matching increase in D1 activity.
    - Possible answer noted: dopamine changes intracellular state / gain / plasticity, while local inhibition and network dynamics shape whether somatic activity actually rises.
    - Circuit sketch: D1 MSNs in NAcc are GABAergic and may carry value-like signals back toward VTA, while dopamine returning to NAcc may carry TD-error-like information.
    - Method note from the paper: somatic rather than axonal calcium was recorded because muscimol may directly inhibit dopamine axons, which express GABA receptors.
