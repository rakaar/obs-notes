# Acetylcholine demixes heterogeneous dopamine signals for learning and moving

#unread

## Why it seems relevant

- Dopamine signals may reflect both reward-prediction-error-like learning signals and movement-related signals.
- The paper argues that acetylcholine helps separate or demix these heterogeneous dopamine signals.
- Relevant for dopamine papers, reinforcement learning, and the learning-vs-movement distinction in striatal circuitry.
