# On the origin of the nucleus: a hypothesis

#read

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10732040/

Related to [[The Vital Question]] — eukaryogenesis and the origin of the nucleus.

Proposes a model for the archaeal origin of the nucleus. Discusses:
- The eukaryotic nucleoplasm and cytoplasm as distinct regions of a common compartment
- Phylogenetic and experimental data showing archaeal counterparts to eukaryotic nuclear machines
- Cell biology of TACK and Asgardarchaeota (closest archaeal relatives of eukaryotes)
- Predictions that can be tested
